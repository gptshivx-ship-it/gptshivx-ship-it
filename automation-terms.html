What this promise covers

The exact form fields, sheet and notification address are written down first in a 1-page plan that you approve.

If your idea is one of those, we will say so upfront and there is no pay-only-when-it-works promise for it; nothing is charged unless you agree a price in writing first. If yours is not this type of build, we will tell you upfront and there is no charge.
