ShivX Labs - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026.

ShivX Labs ("we") provides done-for-you business automation to business customers (see "Services for business customers" below) and operates an internal publishing tool called "ShivX". The publishing tool is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's own performance statistics. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to upload videos to, and read statistics from, the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not access any other person's account.

What we store

The authorisation token is stored on the owner's own computer and is never shared or sold. We do not collect personal information about viewers or any third party.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Enquiry form

If you send us an enquiry through our enquiry form, we receive the name, business name, email address and request text that you type. We use them only to reply to you about that request. They are not sold or shared. We keep a short record that an enquiry arrived (a one-way hash of your email address, not the address itself) so that we do not contact you twice by mistake. Ask us at gptshivx@gmail.com and we will delete your enquiry. Replies to enquiries are written by ShivX Labs' AI assistant.

Services for business customers

When a business engages us to set up an automation (for example a form that adds a row to a spreadsheet and sends an email, appointment or due-date reminders, payment reminders, review requests or enquiry follow-ups), the business shares its own form or spreadsheet with us. We process the data in it (names, email addresses, job, invoice and appointment details) only to run that business's automation. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for any other purpose.

Where the automation emails the business's own contacts, we send those emails on the business's behalf. They are identified as coming from the business ("Business name via ShivX Labs"), replies go to the business, and each says it was sent on the business's behalf. Every such email includes how to unsubscribe: email gptshivx@gmail.com with STOP in the subject, or reply STOP. We honour those requests through a suppression list, so an address that has asked to stop is not emailed again by any of our automations.

We keep a customer's data while the service is active and for 30 days afterwards, then delete it. A business or one of its contacts can ask us to delete their data at any time by emailing gptshivx@gmail.com. Replies and emails are written by ShivX Labs' AI assistant; if you would rather deal with a person, say so and we will arrange it.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
